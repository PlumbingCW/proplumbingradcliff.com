About Rogersville

Rogersville is an older community that was once separate from Radcliff and is now described as part of the city. Wikipedia places it in the southern part of Radcliff, along US 31W and KY 447. A 2024 history of Radcliff covers Rogersville alongside Red Hill, Longview, Stithton and New Stithton, which shows the name has a long local record.

Rogersville Road is a real street in ZIP code 40160, and home listings use it. A data aggregator labels the surrounding area Rogersville and Vine Grove Junction and says it is mostly single-family housing with some mobile homes. That is an aggregator summary and is treated here as unverified.

The edge of the Radcliff city limits near Rogersville is not clearly documented, and another directory lists Rogersville as a nearby community. If you live along US 31W or KY 447 south of the main part of the city, check your mailing address and your utility bill, then call to ask about service.

Which city, which utility

On the edge of a city, the first question is often who is responsible. Hardin County Water District No. 1 was formed in 1953 and is the main supplier of water in the county. The Kentucky Public Service Commission also lists a City of Radcliff sewer tariff filed under that district.

A house along US 31W may be on city sewer, on a septic system or on a different arrangement, and this page cannot tell which. Your water and sewer bills show what applies. For a septic system, a plumber looks at the house side and a septic contractor handles the tank and field.

Plumbing services that suit homes on the highway corridors

Properties spread along a highway often have a longer run of pipe between the road and the house. If a water bill rises with no change in how you use water, repairing the water line and leak detection are the services that look for a break between the meter and the building.

For a slow tub or a toilet that keeps backing up, cleaning the drain clears the line, and a sewer camera look shows what is inside it when cleaning does not hold. A tank that no longer heats is a job for a water heater repair call.
