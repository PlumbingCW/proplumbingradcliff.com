Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026.

Call (855) 575-2890

What we collect

This website has no forms, accounts or chat. We do not collect information through the site itself, apart from basic technical data your browser sends, such as pages visited, device type and approximate location.

When you call the dispatch line, we receive your phone number and whatever details you share, such as your address and the plumbing problem. Calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How call information is used

Call details are used to dispatch a plumber, confirm the work and price, and improve the service. Information may be shared with the licensed plumber assigned to your job so they can reach you and complete the work.

Cookies and analytics

The site may use basic analytics and call-tracking tools that set cookies or read similar technical identifiers. You can block cookies in your browser settings, and the site still works.

Maps and third parties

Pages embed Google Maps. When a map loads, Google may receive technical data from your browser under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about your call or ask for it to be deleted by calling the dispatch line or emailing contact@proplumbingradcliff.com. We do not sell personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
