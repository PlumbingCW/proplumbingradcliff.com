Tankless service: what it is and why it comes up

A tankless water heater heats water on demand as it moves through a heat exchanger, so there is no tank to store it. Tankless service covers the upkeep and repair of that exchanger, its sensors, filters and venting. The most common planned job is a descaling flush, and the most common complaint is an error code.

Nothing about this work is urgent in the usual sense. If your tankless unit is leaking at the cabinet or you smell gas, that is different, and the 24/7 line takes it any hour.

Symptoms that mean the unit needs attention

These signs tend to come before a breakdown. Catch them early and the fix is usually a flush or a filter clean.

  • An error code on the display that returns after a reset.
  • Hot water that runs fine and then goes cold for a few seconds before returning.
  • Weaker hot flow at the tap than you used to get, even though cold flow is strong.
  • Water temperature that drifts up and down without anyone changing a setting.
  • A clicking, rumbling or whistling sound while the unit fires.
  • A longer wait for hot water than the unit used to need.
  • Visible white crust on fittings or the inlet screen.

If you see several of these together, a scale problem in the exchanger is the first suspect.

Safety steps before a technician arrives

If you smell gas near a gas unit, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first. Do not try to relight anything.

For a unit that is leaking or throwing error codes, turn off its power at the switch or breaker. Close the cold-water valve feeding it if you can reach it safely, and keep water use low until a plumber has looked at it. Hot water can scald, so test it with a hand under the cold tap first.

From symptom to cause: the diagnosis path

A plumber works from the cheap, common causes toward the rare ones.

1

Read the code and the history

The manual or display lookup narrows the fault to ignition, flow, temperature or venting.

2

Check the inlet filter and flow

A clogged screen or a half-closed valve cuts flow below the minimum needed to fire the burner.

3

Test the exchanger

Scale shows up as high temperature rise, noise and rising pressure drop across the unit.

4

Verify gas, power and venting

Gas pressure, electrical supply and a clear vent path are tested, since each can mimic an exchanger fault.

5

Look for cross-connections

Hot water that goes lukewarm when another tap opens can mean a mixing valve or a crossover in the house plumbing.

Flush and descale: the job that saves tankless heaters

A flush connects a pump and a hose set to the heater's service valves, then circulates a descaling solution through the exchanger for a set period. The solution dissolves mineral buildup, and clean water rinses it out. The filter on the inlet is cleaned at the same visit.

How often depends on the manufacturer and on your water, so check the manual and ask the plumber. Hardin County Water District No. 1 (HCWD1) says its water is hard because of contact with rocks and rocky soils. It publishes no hardness figure, so test your own tap and ask HCWD1 for its annual water quality report.

Why scale is the enemy in hard water

Mineral scale forms where water gets hottest, which is inside the exchanger. A thin layer insulates the metal, narrows the passages and makes the unit overheat or shut down on an error code. Left alone, scale can ruin the exchanger, and that is the costliest part of the heater. A routine flush is cheap by comparison.

Gas or electric tankless: what differs

The fuel changes which problems appear and who is needed for the work.

FactorGas tanklessElectric tankless
VentingNeeds a sealed, correctly sized ventNone required
SupplyLarger gas line often neededSerious amperage, often a dedicated panel circuit
Typical faultsIgnition, flame sensor, vent blockageElements, flow sensor, tripped breaker
Who else may be neededGas line installer if upsizingAn electrician for the circuit
Power during outagesStill needs electricity for the controlsOff entirely until power returns

The outage row matters here. Ice storms in November 2018 and February 2021 knocked out power in Hardin County, and a tankless heater on gas still needs electricity to run its fan and electronics. A tank heater on gas can often keep heating by pilot.

Service it or replace it

A tankless unit is a bigger investment than a tank, so the repair-or-replace call deserves a clear look.

The unit is under roughly 10 years old, the errors are flow or filter related and a flush brings the temperature rise back to normal.

A sensor, igniter or fan has failed and the exchanger is sound.

The household has outgrown it, and two showers at once overload the flow rating.
